# Coal City Realty 🏡

> **Your Land. Your Legacy. Your South East.**  
> Official landing page & web portal for **Coal City Realty** (`coalcityrealty.com`), operating across Enugu State and the South East of Nigeria (Enugu, Anambra, Imo, Abia, and Ebonyi).

---

## 🌟 Overview

Coal City Realty is a modern, responsive, high-converting real estate website designed with a luxury editorial aesthetic tailored specifically for the Nigerian South East market and the diaspora.

- **Primary Colors**: Deep Forest Green (`#1A3C34`) & Warm Gold (`#C9A84C`)
- **Typography**: *Cormorant Garamond* (editorial serif headings) + *DM Sans* (clean modern body)
- **Currency**: Nigerian Naira (₦) formatted throughout
- **Contact & Inquiries**:
  - Live Contact Form routed directly to `hello@coalcityrealty.com`
  - WhatsApp quick-chat floating widget with pulse animation
  - Support: `support@coalcityrealty.com`

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Sticky Adaptive Navbar**: Transparent on hero, switches smoothly to solid with glassmorphism on scroll; includes full mobile slide-in navigation.
2. **Cinematic Hero**: Ambient zoom hero section with South East regional coverage badge, luxury backdrop, and call-to-action triggers.
3. **Property Search & Filter Bar**: Filter by property type, state (Enugu, Anambra, Imo, Abia, Ebonyi), and price range.
4. **Animated Performance Counters**: Live incrementing statistics (1,200+ Properties, 850+ Families, 5 States, 12 Years).
5. **Featured Property Grid**: 6 curated residential, commercial, and rental properties with specs (beds, baths, sqm), hover elevation, and interactive bookmarking.
6. **Why Choose Us**: Value proposition highlighting verified titles (C of O, Survey Plans), local market expertise, transparent transactions, and 7-day agent support.
7. **3-Step Process Flow**: Visual roadmap from Search to Visit to Closing.
8. **Client Testimonials**: Verified testimonials from clients across Enugu, Anambra, and Imo.
9. **Brand Story / About**: Deep roots in the Coal City, generational wealth creation, and dedicated diaspora services.
10. **Interactive Get-In-Touch Form**: Background AJAX submission direct to `hello@coalcityrealty.com` with error handling, loading states, and fallback options.
11. **Comprehensive Footer**: Social media links, quick navigation, category filters, and legal notices.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Markup & Styling**: Pure semantic HTML5 + Custom CSS3 variables & layout (Grid & Flexbox).
- **Animations**: CSS Keyframes + Intersection Observer API for scroll reveals.
- **Icons**: Inline SVG icons (Lucide & Heroicons inspired).
- **Zero Dependencies**: 100% self-contained, no build steps or heavy frameworks required.

## 🌐 Deployment

You can deploy this site in seconds on any static host:

- **Netlify / Vercel**: Connect your GitHub repository or drag and drop the folder.
- **GitHub Pages**: Go to **Settings** > **Pages** > Select `main` branch > **Save**.
- **Traditional cPanel Hosting**: Upload `index.html` into your `public_html` directory for `coalcityrealty.com`.
