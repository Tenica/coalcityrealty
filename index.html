Our Story

Rooted in the South East, Built for Generational Wealth

Founded in 2013 in the heart of Enugu, Coal City Realty was born from a simple but powerful conviction: that every Igbo family — whether residing in Enugu, the diaspora in London, or beyond — deserves clear, secure, and dignified access to property in the land of their fathers.

Over 12 years, we have grown into the most trusted real estate consultancy across the five South East states. Our team of over 40 licensed professionals combines deep ancestral knowledge of the terrain with the highest standards of modern real estate practice.

We don't just facilitate transactions — we build legacies. Every certificate of occupancy we hand over, every land document we verify, every family we place in a new home is a testament to our commitment to the prosperity of the South East.
